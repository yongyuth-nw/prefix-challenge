Teacher / student guide

  1. Project this page or share one screen. Divide students into 2–4 teams and enter team names.
  2. Each team takes a turn. The teacher reads the question, and the active team discusses and selects A–D within the time limit.
  3. After an answer, read the explanation. Give a short pronunciation / contextual-use prompt before pressing Next.
  4. Every team receives 6 questions in 4-team mode. Scores and ranking update automatically. Use Restart for another game.
